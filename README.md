# 🚇 The Solana Line — A Beginner's Transit Map

> **Ride the Solana Line: 9 stops, pure clarity.**  
> An interactive transit-style blueprint map and educational guide explaining Solana blockchain fundamentals simply for crypto beginners.

Built specifically for the Superteam Earn Bounty: **[Explain Solana to Crypto Beginners](https://superteam.fun/earn/listing/explain-solana-to-crypto-beginners)**.

---

## 🌟 Overview

Solana is the fastest, lowest-cost layer-1 blockchain, but for crypto beginners, terms like *Proof of History, Tower BFT, Sealevel, RPCs, and Mnemonic Seeds* can be overwhelming.

**The Solana Line** takes inspiration from iconic subway transit maps (like the London Tube or NYC Subway) to create a clear, linear, 9-station mental model. Each station represents one core concept or security checkpoint.

---

## 🗺️ The 3 Transit Zones & 9 Stations

### 🟢 Zone 1: Getting Started (Stops 01–03)
- **Stop 01: Wallet** — Your non-custodial digital passport & keychain (e.g. Phantom, Solflare, Backpack).
- **Stop 02: Seed Phrase** — The 12–24 word master key. Stored strictly on physical paper, never typed into websites.
- **Stop 03: Address** — Your public account number safe for sharing with anyone.

### 🟡 Zone 2: How It Works (Stops 04–06)
- **Stop 04: Send** — Broadcast cryptographically signed instructions to the network.
- **Stop 05: Confirmed ~400ms (The Central Interchange)** — Proof of History sub-second settlement and immediate finality.
- **Stop 06: Fee** — Microscopic network costs (less than $0.001 per transaction).

### 🔴 Zone 3: Stay Safe (Stops 07–09)
- **Stop 07: Verify URL** — Avoiding pixel-perfect phishing clones and fake search ads.
- **Stop 08: Test First** — Sending a small $1 test transfer before large transactions.
- **Stop 09: Read & Sign** — Reviewing simulated wallet balance changes before confirming.

## ✨ Features

- 🚇 **Interactive Transit Schematic**: Click any station node or run the automated train ride tour.
- 💡 **Real-World Mental Models**: Every step features a direct, relatable analogy and actionable pro-tips.
- ⚠️ **Signal Warnings**: High-visibility hazard alerts highlighting the #1 mistakes beginners make.
- 📖 **Map Legend & Glossary**: Simple, jargon-free explanations of SOL, Validators, PoH, and RPCs.
- 📐 **Blueprint Aesthetic**: High-contrast architectural schematic styling with Barlow Condensed and JetBrains Mono typography.
- 📋 **GitHub Export Kit**: Built-in modal with ready-to-copy repository names, descriptions, tags, and promotional graphics.
