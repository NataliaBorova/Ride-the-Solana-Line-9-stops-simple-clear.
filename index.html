Zone 2: How It Works

Confirmed ~400ms

The Central Interchange: sub-second finality via Proof of History

Solana validators use Proof of History (a cryptographically verifiable sequential clock) to sequence transactions in slots produced every 400 milliseconds.

Core Rule: True sub-second finality means zero waiting around in mempools for block confirmations.
💡 Everyday Analogy
The central express transit interchange where high-speed maglev trains synchronize instantly without stopping at red lights.
🎯 Actionable Tip
Watch your wallet notification: the moment it says "Confirmed", your transaction is permanent on the ledger.
⚙️ Technical Insight
Tower BFT consensus runs on top of Proof of History SHA-256 state hashes, achieving asynchronous Byzantine agreement at speed of light.
⚠️ Common Mistake to Avoid
Submitting duplicate transactions thinking the first one failed, when it was already settled in 400ms.

Signal warnings

01

Your seed phrase is not a password. It's a master key. No app, exchange, or "support agent" will ever legitimately ask for it.

02

A copycat site looks identical to the real one. Check the URL character by character before connecting your wallet.

03

Free money is the bait, not the gift. Unsolicited "airdrops" asking for a wallet connection are almost always scams.

§3

Map legend

SOL

The native currency — used to pay tiny network fees, or to stake and earn rewards.

Validator

An independent computer that confirms transactions. Thousands run the network together — no single owner.

Proof of History

A shared, built-in clock that lets validators agree fast without stopping to check in with each other.

RPC

The "phone line" your wallet or app uses to talk to the network. Invisible, but every transaction needs one.
